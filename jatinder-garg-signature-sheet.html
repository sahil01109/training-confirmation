Before you lock one in

  1. Pretty is not the goal, repeatable is. A signature you cannot redraw the same way fails at the bank counter, and a mismatch on a cheque or property paper costs you time, not the bank.
  2. Keep two, not seven. One full signature for legal and banking, one short initial for page-by-page initialling. Any more and you will mix them up.
  3. Sign it 200 times on the lines below before you put it on any specimen card. If stroke number 200 does not look like stroke number 5, drop that variant and take a simpler one.
  4. Once it is on a bank record, do not change it. Changing later means updating every bank, every mutual fund, every property file. That is the real cost people forget.
  5. Do not sign your name legibly in plain handwriting. A clearly readable signature is the easiest thing in the world to copy.
